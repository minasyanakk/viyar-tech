About Viyar

Viyar: everything for furniture manufacturing. Board materials, hardware, edging, fronts, artificial stone products and production services.

In business since 2002, the company is an official dealer of Egger, Hettich, Swiss Krono, Kronospan, Cleaf and Franke and develops its own brands: Starke, Muller, Ferro Fiori and Lumine. Boards are processed on Homag Group equipment. ViyarTech is responsible for the IT and software products of this ecosystem.
